# FUSKAMO Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 31 October 2026  
**Last reviewed:** 31 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FUSKAMO collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information when people use the FUSKAMO native mobile application, web application and public website (together, the **Services**).

FUSKAMO is a football talent-discovery and community platform. Features and data practices may differ by app version, region, account type and enabled integrations. We do not describe a feature as active merely because it appears in a product plan. This policy applies to data actually submitted to or generated by the Services.

## 2. Information we may process

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

- **Account and identity information:** email address, account identifier, display name, username, profile photo, password authentication data handled by the authentication provider, account role and account preferences.
- **Profile and football information:** player position, age or age range, country or region, club or academy, playing history, biography, strengths, scouting information and details you choose to publish.
- **Content you submit:** photographs, videos, captions, comments, stories, reels, project submissions, hashtags, profile content and associated metadata. Content may include information about other people; only submit it when you have the necessary rights and permissions.
- **Social interactions:** follows, likes, saves, comments, replies, shares, story views, reactions, reports, blocks and other interactions supported by the version you use.
- **Messages and community activity:** direct or group messages, message requests, group membership, broadcast-channel activity and related delivery/read metadata, where those features are enabled.
- **Scout, coach and club information:** applications, organisation or affiliation, professional contact details and verification information submitted for review.
- **Payment and transaction information:** payment amount, transaction status, payment reference, phone number and reconciliation details for supported payment flows. Payment credentials such as an M-Pesa PIN are not requested by FUSKAMO.
- **Device and technical information:** device or browser type, operating system, app version, IP address, log events, crash diagnostics, notification tokens and security events, to the extent collected by the app, hosting providers or enabled SDKs.
- **Location information:** country, region or location details that you choose to add to a profile or content. We do not claim to collect continuous precise GPS location unless a future feature clearly requests permission and explains its purpose.
- **Support and safety information:** messages sent to support, reports, moderation decisions and information needed to investigate abuse, fraud or security incidents.

We may also receive information from service providers that help authenticate accounts, deliver notifications, process payments, host content, provide analytics or operate the Services.

## 3. How we use information

We use information, where necessary, to:

1. Create and secure accounts and authenticate users.
2. Display profiles, posts, stories, reels, projects and other content according to user settings and access controls.
3. Enable discovery, search, recommendations, feeds and connections between players, scouts, coaches, clubs and fans.
4. Deliver messages, notifications and community features.
5. Review scout applications, player submissions, reports and suspected policy violations.
6. Process supported payments and maintain transaction records.
7. Provide customer support, diagnose failures and improve reliability, accessibility and performance.
8. Detect spam, fraud, impersonation, harassment, unsafe contact and other security threats.
9. Meet legal, regulatory, accounting and legitimate record-keeping obligations.
10. Measure product usage and campaign effectiveness where analytics are enabled and permitted.

We do not sell personal information. We do not use private messages or private account data to build public profiles.

## 4. Public content and visibility

Information you choose to place in a public profile or public post may be visible to other users and, depending on the page and settings, to people who do not have an account. Search engines or other people may copy, cache or share public content.

Private account settings, restricted content, message permissions and moderation controls are intended to limit access, but no online service can guarantee that another person will not capture or redistribute content they are allowed to view.

Do not publish private contact information, identity documents, sensitive personal information or another person's image unless you have a lawful basis and permission to do so.

## 5. Children and young players

FUSKAMO is a football platform and some player profiles may concern young athletes. A parent or legal guardian must create or supervise an account or submission for a child where required by applicable law. Children must not be encouraged to publish personal contact details or communicate privately with unknown adults.

Scouts and other adults must use the platform only for legitimate football-related purposes, must not exploit or harass young players, and must follow applicable safeguarding and child-protection laws.

If you believe a child’s information has been submitted without appropriate authority or consent, contact us at brianlexyl03@gmail.com. We may restrict access while we investigate.

The operator must set and enforce an age-appropriate minimum age and guardian-consent process for each launch jurisdiction before enabling account creation for minors.

FUSKAMO may be used in multiple countries. Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we will use appropriate safeguards for international transfers and comply with applicable local requirements.

## 8. Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including account operation, content delivery, security, dispute resolution and legal or financial record keeping.

When an account is deleted, we will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify associated personal information, subject to:
- information that must be retained by law;
- transaction and audit records needed for legitimate accounting, fraud-prevention or dispute purposes;
- content or records needed to investigate abuse, protect users or enforce our terms; and
- backups that expire through the normal backup-retention cycle.

Public content may remain in another user's messages, screenshots, downloads or reposts after deletion of the original account. We will publish more specific retention periods when operational retention schedules have been verified.
